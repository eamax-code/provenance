Draft for the pilot, pending legal review. This page describes how the service works today. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer, and the final wording may change before paid plans open. Questions: use the contact form.

SEC EDGAR (United States)

Company identity, filings and XBRL financial figures from the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission's EDGAR system, read within the SEC's fair-access rules with an identified user agent. Every filing and figure links to its filing on sec.gov. Provenance is not affiliated with the SEC.

Listings in the EU, the UK and Canada

  • ESMA FIRDS (EU and EEA venues): reproduced with the source acknowledged, as ESMA's terms require. ESMA does not endorse Provenance.
  • FCA FIRDS (UK venues): instrument identity and listing dates, with the FCA acknowledged. The FCA does not endorse Provenance.
  • GLEIF: legal entity identity by LEI, under the CC0 licence.
  • Exchange directories (TMX, CSE and others) are not used: their terms do not allow it without permission.

News headlines in chat forecasts

Forecast answers in chat may read recent headlines to inform an estimate, and cite each one. In production they come from the GDELT Project's open news index, which may be used commercially with attribution. Development copies may use Google News RSS, which is for personal, non-commercial use and is refused in production. Only the headline, publisher, date and link are read, never the article. Headlines are not stored as data or served through the API, and a headline is never treated as a fact.

What is not here

  • No stock prices or market data, no analyst estimates, and no data bought from vendors.
  • No figures for non-US companies' financial statements yet.

Crediting the data

When you show a figure, link it to its source_url. Suggested credit: "Data from SEC EDGAR via Provenance" (or "ESMA FIRDS", "FCA FIRDS", "GLEIF" for listings). Do not suggest that any regulator endorses your product.

Public company intelligence. Built for humans and AI.

Provenance is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by the SEC, ESMA, the FCA or GLEIF. Data comes from the SEC's EDGAR filings, ESMA and FCA FIRDS, and GLEIF. Filing availability, taxonomy coverage and timestamps vary, and a missing field does not mean the company has no such information. Values marked derived are computed by this service. Not investment advice; review the filing before relying on a value. © 2026 Provenance.
