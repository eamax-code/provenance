Draft for the pilot, pending legal review. This page describes how the service works today. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer, and the final wording may change before paid plans open. Questions: use the contact form.

In short

We collect the least we need to run the service: your email if you make an account or ask for a key, your keys (stored only as hashes), a record of the requests each key makes, and the conversations you save in chat. We do not sell personal data, show adverts, or use third-party analytics or tracking cookies.

What we collect

  • Account: your email, when the account was made and when you last signed in, and the theme you chose.
  • API keys: a hash of each key, its prefix, the name you gave it, its plan and when it was last used. We cannot see or recover a key after showing it once.
  • Usage: for each request made with a key, the time, the endpoint, the response status and size. Query strings and response bodies are not stored.
  • Key requests: the email, intended use and plan you enter in the request form.
  • Watches: the webhook address or email you ask us to deliver to, and a record of each delivery.
  • Chat: with your own key, the questions and answers of conversations you keep, so you can return to them. With the demo key, nothing is saved on our side.
  • Server logs: request id, path, status, timing and the network address, used for security and rate limits.

Cookies and browser storage

  • prv_session: keeps you signed in to your account. HttpOnly, sent only to this site, for up to 30 days or until you sign out.
  • company_layout: remembers the company page layout you picked.
  • Browser storage (not sent to us): your theme, and in chat and the Explorer the key you connected, so you do not have to paste it again. Disconnect or clear site data to remove it.
  • No advertising or analytics cookies.

Why we use it

To provide the service you asked for (sign-in, keys, delivery of alerts), to enforce plan limits and prevent abuse, to keep the service secure, and to understand which parts of the API are used so we can improve them.

Who else handles it

Our hosting provider and database host store the data; an email provider sends sign-in links and alerts. [Owner: list each provider and its region once tickets 02 and 06 are decided.] Chat answers are written by a language model run on our own infrastructure today; if that changes (GH #13), this section will name the provider before it does.

How long we keep it

  • Request records: 90 days.
  • Delivery records: 90 days after the last attempt.
  • Sign-in links: they expire after 15 minutes; used and expired links are deleted with other old records.
  • Saved chats: until you delete them or close your account.
  • Account and key records: while your account is open, then deleted on request, except where we must keep a record of a revoked key to stop it being reused.

Your choices

You can see your keys and usage on your account page, revoke keys, delete saved chats, and sign out of every device. To get a copy of your data or have your account deleted, use the contact form from the email on the account. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under laws such as the GDPR or India's DPDP Act; we will honour them.
