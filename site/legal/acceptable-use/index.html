Draft for the pilot, pending legal review. This page describes how the service works today. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer, and the final wording may change before paid plans open. Questions: use the contact form.

Built for

  • Products, research, analysis and AI agents that use public company information and cite it.
  • Alerts, dashboards, screens and models over the figures, within your plan's limits.

Not allowed

  • Getting around rate limits or quotas, for example with many free accounts, shared keys or rotating addresses.
  • Copying the dataset wholesale to offer it as a competing service, or reselling raw access without an agreement.
  • Presenting Provenance, the SEC, ESMA, the FCA, GLEIF or an exchange as endorsing you, or removing the filing links in a way that misleads people about where a figure came from.
  • Using the data to mislead investors, to manipulate a market, or in breach of securities law.
  • Probing, scanning or attacking the service, other users, or the regulators' systems through it; webhooks that point at systems you do not control.
  • Sending personal data of others through chat or watch destinations without a lawful basis.

AI agents

Agents using the API or the MCP server must keep the same limits as people, and should cite the filing link for every figure they state. An agent acting for you uses your key, and you are responsible for it.

Reporting a problem

To report a security issue, abuse, or a wrong figure, use the contact form. Please include the request id from the response when you have one.
