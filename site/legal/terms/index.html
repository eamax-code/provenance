The service

Provenance gives developers, analysts and AI agents structured access to public company information: SEC filings and the financial figures reported in them, and listing events in Canada, the UK and the EU. You reach it through the API, the MCP server, the Financial Chat, the Company Explorer and this website.

Provenance is independent. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission, ESMA, the UK Financial Conduct Authority, GLEIF or any exchange.

Accounts and keys

  • You sign in with a link sent to your email. You are responsible for what is done with your account and your API keys.
  • Keep keys secret: use them from a server, not in a web page or a public repository. Revoke a key at once on your account page if it may have leaked.
  • One person or organisation per account. Do not share keys to get around plan limits.

Plans and limits

Each plan has a rate limit, a monthly request quota, an export row limit and a number of watches, shown on the pricing section and returned by /v1/public/plans. The free plan and the public demo key may change or be withdrawn. During the pilot, paid plans are arranged directly; no card is charged through the site.

What the data is, and is not

  • Values are as the companies reported them to the regulator, mapped to standard metrics by published rules. Each value links to the filing it came from. Values marked derived are computed by Provenance.
  • A missing value means the filings we loaded did not contain it, not that it does not exist. Coverage, timing and completeness depend on the regulators' own publication.
  • Nothing here is investment, legal, tax or accounting advice, and nothing is a recommendation to buy or sell. Forecasts in chat are estimates computed from filings and headlines; they are not reported figures.
  • Review the underlying filing before relying on a value.

Your use of the data

You may use what the service returns in your own products, research and analysis, including for commercial purposes, within your plan's limits and the acceptable use rules. Keep the filing link when you show a figure to others, and do not present Provenance or a regulator as endorsing your product. Bulk redistribution of the dataset as a substitute for the service needs a written agreement. The regulators' own terms for their data also apply; see data sources.

Availability and changes

The service is provided as it is, during a pilot. We aim to keep it running and fresh, but we do not promise a level of availability or a delay between a filing and its arrival here. The API changes under the versioning policy: changes within /v1 are additive, and breaking changes are announced first.

Liability

To the extent the law allows, Provenance is not liable for losses from using or being unable to use the service, or from decisions made with its data. [Owner and counsel: set the liability cap, the governing law and the courts before paid plans open.]

Suspension and ending

You can stop at any time by revoking your keys. We may suspend a key or an account that breaks these terms or the acceptable use rules, or that puts the service or the regulators' systems at risk, and will tell you why when we can.
