Every figure. Every answer. Traceable to its source.

Don't just get the number. Every figure carries what you need to find it in the original filing.

  • Original filing and date

    The 10-K or 10-Q, the day it was filed, and a link to its index page on sec.gov.

  • Accession number

    The SEC's own ID for the filing, so you can cite it or match it in your systems.

  • XBRL tag

    The exact tag the company reported the value under.

  • Fiscal period

    Fiscal year or quarter, with the start and end dates the filing gives.

  • Reported or derived

    Whether the value is as filed or computed by us. Derived values say how.

  • Mapping version

    The version of the rules that matched the tag to the metric, so a change never goes unseen.

Straight answers

Is this market data?

No. There are no prices, quotes or trading signals anywhere in the product. It covers SEC filings and the financial figures companies report in them.

Where do the figures come from?

From the XBRL financial data inside each company's 10-K and 10-Q filings. Every value keeps the filing's accession number and its XBRL tag, and links to the filing's page on sec.gov.

How fresh is fresh?

Watched companies are checked every five minutes by default. The freshness endpoint reports the last successful fetch and when the latest filing was processed, and it only gives a latency figure when the SEC provides an exact acceptance time to compare against.

Which companies and metrics are covered?

Five metrics from standard us-gaap tags, for the companies loaded here. Looking up a CIK loads a new company on request. IFRS filers and custom extension tags are not covered yet, and a missing value means the filing did not report one we could map.

What does the chat do?

It answers questions from the filings stored here, cites every figure, and checks each number against the data before showing the answer. Lists across companies, such as recent IPOs, who filed a form or rankings, come back as tables. A model running on this server writes it; your question is not sent to an outside AI service.

Can I redistribute the data?

Everything comes from SEC filings and each row links to its filing. Before building a bulk redistribution product on top, read our data-rights notes and get your own legal review; we document the open questions rather than pretend they are settled.

How do webhooks stay trustworthy?

Each watch gets its own secret. Deliveries carry a timestamp and an HMAC-SHA256 signature over the body, plus a stable event ID for de-duplication. We never follow redirects or deliver to private network addresses.
